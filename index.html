    House rules

    Nine simple ones. Tap any to read it. Short version: be kind, keep it in English.

    Don’t disrespect others

    No discrimination, insults, harassment or hate speech. azraq is a toxic-free space.

    Use content warnings

    Add CW/TW and spoiler tags for triggering topics. Staff may remove anything harmful.

    English only

    Keep chats in English so staff can moderate properly.

    No inappropriate or NSFW content

    No nudity, gore or explicit stuff in chat, avatars, statuses or profile banners.

    No advertising or self-promotion

    No DM ads, server or stream links, art commissions, or selling game accounts.

    No spamming or mass pings

    Don’t flood text, images or emojis, and don’t mass ping members or staff.

    No threats, slurs or political debates

    Heated talk about politics and controversial topics isn’t allowed.

    No toxicity

    Friendly banter is fine. If someone asks you to stop, stop.

    Keep personal info private

    No real names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, passwords or private socials, yours or anyone else’s.

    Thanks, you’re all set! Come join us, then hit #verify.

    Questions?

    The quick answers.

    Can I wear the server tag?

    Yes! Pick the aeon tag in your Discord profile settings and it shows next to your name.

    How do I get in?

    Join with the invite, then press the button in #verify to unlock the rest of the server.

    How do I get roles?

    Game roles, color roles and self roles each have their own channel in the Self section.

    Can I share my stream or art link?

    Not in chat, DMs or voice, since advertising is against the rules. Show your work in #creations instead.

    How do I become a booster?

    Boost the server from the server menu in Discord and the booster channels unlock for you.

    Someone is breaking the rules. What now?

    Message a staff member in the server and they’ll take a look.

    Want to help out?

    Staff applications live in #staff-app inside the server. Check the requirements first and see if you’re ready.
